Constitution

Name

Kurdish Women Society

Purpose

To support, empower, and connect Kurdish women in the UK and internationally through wellbeing, culture, confidence, and access to opportunities.

Aims

  • Create safe, women-only spaces
  • Support wellbeing and confidence
  • Celebrate Kurdish culture
  • Remove financial barriers
  • Facilitate access to external programmes
  • Build international community

Membership

  • Open to all Kurdish women aged 16+
  • Free membership unless decided otherwise
  • Members uphold values of respect and safety

Committee

  • Chair
  • Secretary
  • Treasurer

Meetings

  • Held at least twice per year
  • Decisions by majority vote

Finance

  • Treasurer manages funds
  • Funds must support society aims
  • May apply for grants and donations

Safeguarding

Committed to providing safe spaces for women.

Dissolution

Remaining funds donated to a Kurdish women’s charity.
