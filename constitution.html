Constitution

Name

Kurdish Women Society

Purpose

To support, empower, and connect Kurdish women in the UK and internationally through wellbeing, culture, confidence, and access to opportunities.

Aims

  1. To create safe, women only spaces for Kurdish women to meet, grow, and build confidence.
  2. To support wellbeing, movement, and active lifestyles through simple, accessible activities.
  3. To celebrate Kurdish culture, heritage, and identity.
  4. To remove financial, social, and cultural barriers that prevent Kurdish women from accessing opportunities.
  5. To act as a facilitator by securing grants and funding to help Kurdish women enrol in external programmes such as football training, fitness classes, educational courses, and personal development workshops.
  6. To provide community support networks that strengthen confidence, leadership, and social belonging.
  7. To build international connections between Kurdish women worldwide.

Membership

  • Open to all Kurdish women aged 16+.
  • Free membership unless otherwise decided by the committee.
  • Members agree to uphold the society’s values of respect, safety, and community.

Committee

The society will be led by a small committee consisting of:

  • Chair
  • Secretary
  • Treasurer

Additional voluntary roles may be added as needed (e.g., Events Lead, Wellbeing Lead, Culture Lead).

Meetings

  • Committee meetings held at least twice per year.
  • Decisions made by simple majority vote.
  • Members may be invited to open meetings.

Finance

  • The Treasurer manages all funds.
  • All spending must support the aims of the society.
  • The society may apply for grants, donations, and sponsorships.
  • No committee member may personally profit from society funds.

Safeguarding

The society is committed to providing safe spaces for women. Any activity involving young people or vulnerable adults will follow appropriate safeguarding guidance.

Dissolution

If the society closes, remaining funds will be donated to a Kurdish women’s charity or community organisation with similar aims.
