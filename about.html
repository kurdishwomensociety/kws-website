About Us

The Kurdish Women Society is a community organisation dedicated to empowering Kurdish women through wellbeing, sport, art, culture, confidence, and access to opportunities.

We create safe, women-only spaces where Kurdish women can connect, grow, and feel supported through social gatherings, cultural events, wellbeing activities, and funded access to external training programmes such as football, fitness, education, and personal development.

Our society is open to Kurdish women of all ages, backgrounds, and experiences in the UK and internationally.

Our Purpose

To build a strong, supportive, international community of Kurdish women who feel confident, connected, and empowered to access opportunities they may not have had before.

Our Vision

A world where Kurdish women move freely, learn boldly, lead visibly, and celebrate their identity with pride supported by a global network of sisters.

Our Mission

  • To create safe spaces for Kurdish women
  • To support wellbeing, confidence, and personal growth
  • To celebrate Kurdish culture and identity
  • To remove financial barriers to opportunities
  • To help women access external training programmes
  • To build a strong international community of Kurdish women

What We Do

Community & Wellbeing

  • Women-only gatherings
  • Confidence-building activities

Culture & Identity

  • Kurdish cultural events
  • Heritage storytelling
  • Kurdish women’s history and leadership

Opportunity Access (Funding Support)

We act as a facilitator, securing grants to help Kurdish women enrol in external programmes such as:

  • Football training
  • Fitness classes
  • Educational courses
  • Leadership workshops
  • Personal development programmes

We do not run the training ourselves; we remove barriers so women can join existing, high-quality programmes delivered by qualified coaches and providers.
